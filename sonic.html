Sonic the Hedgehog x OddFellows Ice Cream

Lead Artist & Creative Concept

August 2023

A collaboration between iconic video game publisher SEGA of America and Brooklyn-based ice cream franchise OddFellows Ice Cream as a promotion for their global franchise mascot Sonic the Hedgehog. As a longtime fan with a wealth of existing franchise knowledge, I conceptualized a collection of 4 flavors that would pay homage to the long-running series of media and feel unique, specific, and cohesive as a collection. I created digital and in-store assets, handled all product photography, as well as packaging design.

Packaging

This collection's concept was geared towards paying tribute to Sonic's classic roots on the original SEGA Genesis. Each flavor is based on the most iconic levels from each of the titles from the original video game series' run paired with each of the iconic character's famous friends, introduced in each of the respective titles.


The flavor titles styled after the level transition screen from their respective game (i.e. the beginning of any given level in the “Sonic the Hedgehog 2” game defined the appearance of the “Tales of Marshmallow Skies” label flavor title).

New Artwork

Original Assets

Artwork for the project was directly interpolated from select, recognizable objects and backgrounds found throughout their respective levels, and then arranged into patterns. The result is that each label is immediately recognizable while still maintaining the mid-century, patterned design language of the OddFellows brand.
